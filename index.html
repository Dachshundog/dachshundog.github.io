Research

Three Connected Directions

Human Mobility

"How do daily schedules and household obligations shape urban travel?"

My own school commutes and later move to Beijing made travel time and accessibility tangible questions. I want to understand how household responsibilities and spatial constraints organise everyday movement.

  • Daily travel patterns & recurring destinations
  • Coupling between school journeys, work commutes, and daily activities

Activity Chains

"What behavioural patterns can we infer from coarse, uncertain signalling data?"

Working with coarse signalling records led me to ask what activity information remains useful when locations are uncertain. I aim to preserve recurring structures, household coordination and individual variation without requiring precise reconstruction of every movement.

  • Bayesian activity inference & sequence analysis
  • Household travel coordination under signalling uncertainty

GeoAI & Site Selection

"How can spatial intelligence evaluate urban accessibility and guide location decisions?"

Public-space assessment and mapping projects made me interested in how places function within daily activity systems. I aim to connect heterogeneous geographic evidence to location decisions, while accounting for differences in data availability and quality across cities.

  • POI & footfall analytics, commercial catchments
  • Service coverage & route evaluation via geospatial data and Google Maps APIs

Activity Semantics and School-Related Travel Chains

How school journeys and work commutes are coordinated within household activity chains — using Bayesian inference on mobile phone signalling to capture overlapping behavioural tendencies across a full month of observations.

Methods
