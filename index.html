About

Jiahe Xi 郗家禾

I am a master's student at the School of Architecture and Landscape Design, Peking University. My research combines mobile phone signalling and travel surveys to understand activity semantics and school-related travel chains. At Master Concept, I work on mobility data integration, POI-based spatial analysis and route evaluation.

Research

Understanding daily travel through activities and places

My work connects travel behaviour analysis with geospatial modelling, from interpreting individual stops to assessing urban service provision and commercial catchments.

Human Mobility

Daily travel patterns, recurring destinations and the relationships between school journeys, work commutes and other activities.

Activity Chains

Bayesian activity inference, sequence analysis and household travel coordination, with explicit attention to uncertainty in signalling records.

GeoAI & Site Selection

POI and footfall analysis, commercial catchments, service coverage and route evaluation using geospatial data and Google Maps APIs.

Master's thesis · 2025–2026

Activity Semantics and School-Related Travel Chains

I examine how school journeys and work commutes are coordinated within household activity chains, including how later commitments constrain earlier journeys. Stop timing, duration, recurrence and sequence position inform Bayesian activity inference. Month-long mobility profiles capture overlapping behavioural tendencies and dependencies between daily activities.

Data
Mobile phone signalling and travel-survey diaries
Methods
Bayesian activity inference and soft mobility profiling
Focus

Mobility analysis and spatial applications

Jun 2026–present

Map AI Solutions Engineer Intern

Master Concept · Google Maps Platform reseller · Hong Kong

I work with developers to integrate mobility and site-selection APIs, validate sample trajectories and derive footfall and delivery-service attributes from POI and trajectory data. I configure Gemini Enterprise agents to query POI and footfall data through the Places Insights API, supporting analysis of commercial catchments and service gaps. Using SQL and BigQuery, I compare routes by distance, travel time and coverage under vehicle and stop constraints.

2025

Urban Public Space Assessment and Park Service Analysis

Research Assistant, Peking University · Hunan CSPON Programme

I led data collection and street-view image analysis, using semantic segmentation, depth estimation and object detection to measure greenery, openness and facility visibility. I also modelled park service provision using visitation flows and distance decay, incorporating supply capacity and demand estimates, and prepared maps of spatial variation in service provision.
