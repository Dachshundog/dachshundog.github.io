Peking University · Master’s researcher

Jiahe Xi

Human Mobility · Activity Chains · GeoAI

I am a master’s student at the School of Architecture and Landscape Design, Peking University, with expected graduation in January 2027. I study how people organise daily activities and how those activities shape urban travel.

My research combines mobile phone signalling and travel surveys to infer activity semantics, represent uncertainty and examine school-related travel chains and household coordination.

At Master Concept, I work on POI-based spatial analysis, mobility data integration and route evaluation. I am interested in how GeoAI can inform accessibility, commercial catchments and location decisions.

Research interests

Human Mobility

How do daily schedules and household obligations shape urban travel?

My school commutes and move to Beijing made travel time and accessibility tangible questions. I want to understand how spatial constraints and household responsibilities organise everyday movement.

Activity Chains

What can we infer from coarse, uncertain signalling data?

I aim to recover useful activity semantics while preserving uncertainty, recurring structures and individual variation across days and household members.

GeoAI & Site Selection

How can geographic evidence guide location decisions?

Public-space assessment and mapping projects motivate me to connect heterogeneous spatial evidence to accessibility and service provision, including in data-scarce settings.
