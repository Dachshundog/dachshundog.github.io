Jiahe Xi

Human Mobility · Spatial Supply & Demand · Site Selection & Traffic Operations

I am a Master of Engineering student at the School of Architecture and Landscape Design, Peking University. My research focuses on spatiotemporal intelligence, smart cities and human mobility. I am interested in understanding the spatial and functional structure of cities through the organisation of everyday activities at the individual level.

My research combines large-scale mobile phone signalling data with travel surveys to infer habitual activity patterns from coarse, uncertain observations. I investigate how individual activity chains aggregate into citywide mobility networks and reveal the places that anchor urban life.

At Master Concept, I work as a Mobility Solution and AI Development Engineer, developing transferable spatial intelligence solutions built on Google Maps Places Insights and heterogeneous geographic data. My work focuses on extracting actionable consumer profiles, understanding spatial demand, and supporting site-selection and transportation decisions across cities. I am also developing a GeoAI database solution for a telecommunications company to support network optimisation, unlock the value of mobility data, and enable privacy-preserving spatial analytics.

Research Interests

Human Mobility

How do individual activity patterns shape citywide mobility networks?

I investigate recurring activity anchors and habitual routines around workplaces, schools and homes. By connecting individual activity chains inferred from large-scale mobile signalling data, I aim to uncover collective flow networks and identify the urban places that organise everyday movement.

Spatial Supply & Demand

Where does urban service provision diverge from people's everyday needs?

I examine how the spatial distribution of jobs, schools, transport and commercial services relates to activity-based demand. By identifying accessibility gaps and spatial mismatches, I seek to inform more efficient and equitable allocation of urban services and infrastructure.

Site Selection & Traffic Operations

How can spatial intelligence improve location decisions and transportation system operations?

I develop transferable spatial intelligence methods that integrate population, POIs, mobility patterns and transport networks. My work connects activity-based demand with site selection, network planning and traffic operations, supporting evidence-based decisions across cities with varying data availability.

My research aims to reconstruct the spatial organisation of urban life from multi-source mobility data and apply these insights to improve urban resource allocation and transportation systems.
