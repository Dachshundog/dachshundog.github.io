Public Space & Park Service Analysis

Research Assistant · Peking University · Hunan CSPON Programme

Research motivation

How do the qualities of places relate to the services people receive?

Public-space assessment made me recognise that describing a place’s physical characteristics is only part of understanding it. I am interested in how places are connected through people’s activities and how those connections relate to accessibility and service provision.

Work & methods

  • Led data collection and street-view image analysis using semantic segmentation, depth estimation and object detection.
  • Measured greenery, spatial openness and facility visibility and contributed to public-space quality indicators.
  • Modelled park service provision using visitation flows, distance decay, supply capacity and demand estimates.
  • Prepared maps of spatial variation in service provision and contributed to methods, results and the patent report.
